# Business Logic

## Diagnosis Pattern

Use this sequence:

1. 现象: what the data shows.
2. 判断: whether the result is healthy, risky, or incomplete.
3. 证据: the few numbers, customers, SKUs, or leads that prove it.
4. 原因: what likely drove the result.
5. 动作: what the boss should inspect next month.

## UFS Monthly Review Questions

- Sales: 本月销售是否达成? Compared with last month, is growth real or only caused by a few customers?
- Targets: 销售、活跃客户、新开客户、家乐目标分别完成到什么程度?
- Customers: 客户池是不是变厚? 高价值客户是否增加? 休眠/下滑客户是否被处理?
- Jiale/SKU: 家乐是不是从单点爆发走向更多客户和更多 SKU?
- Visits/leads: 拜访是否形成建议跟进、持续观察、成交待转出?
- Next month: 哪几件动作最值得老板盯?

## Customer Categories

- 休眠唤回: high urgency. Explain lost opportunity and required recovery action.
- 下滑挽回: high urgency. Explain decline and required protection action.
- 新客培育: growth seed. Explain whether new customers are being converted to repeat purchase.
- 上升拓展: expansion opportunity. Explain what to add: SKU, frequency, or category.
- 稳定维护: base. Keep it simple unless the customer is high value.

## Value Tiers

Use system-provided `value_tier` first. If missing, propose a threshold only as an assumption.

For PPT, avoid showing too many tiers. Use 大客 / 骨干 / 中小 / 尾部 when available.

## Jiale / SKU Logic

Do not say "Jiale is good" only because sales is high. Diagnose:

- Jiale sales amount
- Jiale share
- Jiale change vs previous period
- Jiale customer coverage
- Jiale SKU depth
- Top Jiale SKU concentration

If growth is concentrated in one SKU or one customer, mark it as "亮眼但可复制性待验证".

## Lead / Visit Logic

Use leads as action evidence, not as a generic visit count.

- 建议跟进: enter next-month action list.
- 成交待转出: check whether converted to formal customer/order.
- 持续观察: keep in visit rhythm.
- 暂不跟进: do not overemphasize in boss deck.

Do not calculate visit completion rate unless a reliable visit plan target exists.

## Terminal Store Advancement Logic

Use this for 西安耀盛-style strategic co-creation decks.

Diagnose the model through 人、客、品、配:

- 人: salesperson profile, learning ability, visit discipline, meeting/review rhythm.
- 客: target customer type, lead source, customer development path, customer tier.
- 品: opening product, Jiale product, product portfolio, SKU implantation.
- 配: delivery frequency, service promise, order fulfillment, efficiency bottleneck.

Use four efficiency lenses:

- 拜访效率: visit volume, effective visit, visit conversion, follow-up rhythm.
- 客户效率: active customer, repeat purchase, customer output, customer tier movement.
- 产品效率: Jiale/SKU penetration, SKU depth, product mix.
- 配送效率: delivery mode, delivery workload, service cost and stability.

For strategic decks, the conclusion should align on a scalable operating model, not only summarize monthly KPI.
