# UFS Component Library

Use this file when selecting reusable UFS page components from the three UFS reference decks and the cherry-red logic library.

## Component Selection Rules

- Use UFS source slides for business structure and wording.
- Use cherry-red slides for visual logic only after the business page purpose is clear.
- Before choosing a cherry-red slide by page number, read `component-cards.md` and select by business question: result, cause, structure, path, or accountability.
- Prefer a UFS source component when the page needs UFS-specific concepts such as 生意合伙人, 客户开发, 家乐, 2+2拜访, 人客品配, 四大效率.
- Prefer a cherry-red component when the page needs a cleaner logic diagram or KPI/driver layout.
- Do not combine too many component styles in one deck. Pick one visual base, then borrow structure carefully.

## 视觉来源优先级

- 南充月会复盘 deck 优先。
- 西安共创会 deck 次之，用于工作坊、终端单店进阶、人客品配模型、拜访模型、地图和落地推进计划。
- 樱桃红逻辑组件排第三，只作为复杂逻辑页的局部增强。
- 自建可编辑形状排最后，只在没有合适源页结构时使用。
- 保持 UFS/联合利华饮食策划的橙红经营复盘风格：白底、密集表格、橙色表头、浅橙底纹、红框标注、朴素项目标题，以及从源 deck 继承的页脚标识。
- 不要把每一页都改造成干净的咨询式卡片布局。月会页经常应该像经营证据页，而不是抽象高管页。

## 模板模式映射

- 封面 / 开场 / Q&A：使用南充 slide 1 和结尾页作为母版模式。
- 核心指标回顾和目标达成：优先使用南充 4 月 / 6 月 slide 2。
- 目标达成明细：优先使用南充 KPI 目标表格风；只有需要更强进度视觉时，才局部参考樱桃红 slide 12。
- 增长来源：优先使用南充 6 月 slide 5 的业务结构；樱桃红 slide 46 只用于增强驱动拆解逻辑。
- 客户结构和层级迁移：优先使用南充 4 月 slides 4-5、南充 6 月 slide 4；樱桃红 slide 87 只用于增强迁移表达。
- 家乐专项和 SKU 做深：优先使用南充 6 月家乐 / SKU 页面；樱桃红 slide 59 只作为阶段路径的局部增强。
- 拜访和商机：优先使用南充 4 月 slide 7 的客户开发周期，或西安 slides 9-11 的拜访模型；樱桃红 slide 111 只在需要紧凑效果曲线时使用。
- 上月动作复盘：使用南充动作复盘表格页，不要强套樱桃红组件。
- 下月行动计划：优先使用南充月会结尾 / 行动表格风；只有需要更结构化行动板时，才局部参考樱桃红 slide 113。

## Monthly Review Components

### 1. Core KPI Review

- Source: `nanchong-april-review.pptx` slide 2; `nanchong-june-review.pptx` slide 2.
- Use for: 核心指标回顾 / 月度开场.
- Answers: 本月经营是否有增长? 客户开发、活跃客户、家乐是否同步改善?
- Needs: sales, active customers, new customers, customer pool, Jiale sales, Jiale share, previous month values.
- Replace with: current month vs previous month cards; one headline conclusion.
- Risk: avoid showing every metric. Keep to the few that change owner judgment.

### 2. Target Achievement

- Source: `nanchong-april-review.pptx` slide 2; cherry-red slide 12.
- Use for: 目标达成情况.
- Answers: 目标完成到什么程度? 哪些指标超预期?
- Needs: actual, target, completion rate for sales, active customers, new customers, Jiale if available.
- Replace with: target ladder or target-vs-actual cards.
- Risk: target page explains result only; follow with growth source.

### 3. Profitability / Expansion Feasibility

- Source: `nanchong-april-review.pptx` slide 3.
- Use for: 盈利模拟 / 团队扩张可行性.
- Answers: 当前品类和销售结构是否支撑人员扩张?
- Needs: gross margin by SKU/category, sales amount, people cost, expected output.
- Replace with: conservative profit model and "can expand / cannot expand" conclusion.
- Risk: do not use without margin or cost assumptions.

### 4. Customer Value Structure

- Source: `nanchong-april-review.pptx` slides 4-5.
- Use for: 客户结构分析.
- Answers: 销售贡献来自哪个客户层? 新开客户是否进入贡献主力?
- Needs: customer monthly sales, value tiers, new/old customer flag, contribution rate.
- Replace with: value bands such as 5000+, 1000-5000, 500-1000, 0-500.
- Risk: if thresholds differ by project, state threshold source.

### 5. Quantity-Profit Product Analysis

- Source: `nanchong-april-review.pptx` slide 6.
- Use for: 量利分析 / SKU质量.
- Answers: 哪些SKU既有量又有利润? 家乐是否贡献利润质量?
- Needs: SKU sales, gross margin, margin tier, profit contribution.
- Replace with: high/mid/low margin SKU groups.
- Risk: UFS current system may not have margin; if margin unavailable, replace with SKU contribution page.

### 6. Customer Development Cycle

- Source: `nanchong-april-review.pptx` slide 7.
- Use for: 新客开发复盘 / 拜访节奏.
- Answers: 从陌拜到首单需要多久? 成功开发靠一次成交还是持续培育?
- Needs: first visit date, first order date, revisit dates, sales contribution.
- Replace with: median conversion days, revisit interval, post-order follow-up.
- Risk: needs complete visit records.

### 7. Last-Month Action Review

- Source: `nanchong-june-review.pptx` action recap pages.
- Use for: 上月动作复盘.
- Answers: 上月动作是否执行? 哪些动作产生结果?
- Needs: previous action list, execution notes, customer/SKU/task evidence.
- Replace with: action -> execution -> result -> next decision.
- Risk: do not summarize as "基本执行" without evidence.

### 8. Jiale Special Review

- Source: `nanchong-june-review.pptx`; cherry-red slide 59.
- Use for: 家乐专项 / SKU做深.
- Answers: 家乐增长来自客户覆盖、SKU深度还是个别脉冲?
- Needs: Jiale sales, Jiale share, Jiale SKU list, customer coverage, repeat purchase.
- Replace with: trial -> repeat -> multi-SKU -> key-customer deepening.
- Risk: if concentrated in one customer/SKU, say "亮眼但可复制性待验证".

### 9. Growth Source Breakdown

- Source: cherry-red slide 46; UFS monthly review KPI pages.
- Use for: 销售增长来源.
- Answers: 增长来自客户数、客户单产、SKU、家乐还是大客户?
- Needs: customer-level sales, previous/current amount, SKU/category contribution.
- Replace with: center as monthly growth; branches as customer/order/product/Jiale.
- Risk: cannot be honest with summary-only data.

### 10. Next-Month Action Plan

- Source: UFS monthly review closing pages; cherry-red slide 113.
- Use for: 下月行动计划.
- Answers: 老板下月抓哪几件事?
- Needs: action theme, target customer/SKU/channel, owner, metric, deadline.
- Replace with: 3-5 inspectable action lines.
- Risk: avoid generic slogans; every action needs a metric.

## Strategic Co-Creation Components

### 11. Co-Creation Agenda

- Source: `xian-yaosheng-co-creation.pptx` slide 2.
- Use for: 共创会目录.
- Answers: 会议将围绕什么形成共识?
- Needs: meeting objective and sections.
- Replace with: 推进回顾与现状诊断 / 战略方向与目标共识 / 落地与推进计划.
- Risk: do not use for normal monthly review.

### 12. Progress Review And Current Diagnosis

- Source: `xian-yaosheng-co-creation.pptx` slides 3-5.
- Use for: 项目推进回顾与现状诊断.
- Answers: 项目跑到哪一步? 哪些能力已形成? 哪些还未固化?
- Needs: before/after progress, diagnosis notes, capability gaps.
- Replace with: project start condition -> current progress -> gaps.
- Risk: needs qualitative consultant judgment, not only KPI.

### 13. Long-Period Data Overview

- Source: `xian-yaosheng-co-creation.pptx` slide 6.
- Use for: 6-12个月核心数据总览.
- Answers: 终端单店进阶是否有长期改善?
- Needs: historical sales, active customers, Jiale sales, before/after baseline.
- Replace with: period comparison or trend summary.
- Risk: not suitable for one-month data only.

### 14. Single-Person Operating Model

- Source: `xian-yaosheng-co-creation.pptx` slide 7.
- Use for: 单兵作战模型 / 业务闭环.
- Answers: 一个人怎么跑通终端单店业务?
- Needs: people, customer, product, delivery model, tracking mechanism.
- Replace with: 人客品配 + 四大效率.
- Risk: do not overuse if deck is pure monthly review.

### 15. Salesperson Profile

- Source: `xian-yaosheng-co-creation.pptx` slide 8.
- Use for: 人员画像 / 能力要求.
- Answers: 什么样的人适合做终端单店?
- Needs: salesperson qualities, capability assessment, training evidence.
- Replace with: current team vs ideal profile.
- Risk: keep it respectful and action-oriented.

### 16. Visit Script And Key Process

- Source: `xian-yaosheng-co-creation.pptx` slide 9.
- Use for: 拜访关键环节 / 话术.
- Answers: 拜访如何从介绍走到需求挖掘和成交?
- Needs: visit process, script examples, conversion metrics.
- Replace with: opening, need discovery, objection handling, product recommendation.
- Risk: too text-heavy; use appendix if detailed scripts are long.

### 17. Single Visit Planning

- Source: `xian-yaosheng-co-creation.pptx` slide 10.
- Use for: 单次拜访规划.
- Answers: 一天怎么安排陌拜、线索跟进、存量维护?
- Needs: visit time windows, workload, conversion expectation.
- Replace with: time block + customer type + expected result.
- Risk: do not present benchmarks as universal if local context differs.

### 18. 2+2 Visit Model

- Source: `xian-yaosheng-co-creation.pptx` slide 11.
- Use for: 2+2拜访模式.
- Answers: 如何提高开发转化率?
- Needs: visit sequence, follow-up cycle, lead stage, first-order timing.
- Replace with: first two days broad development, next two days focused follow-up.
- Risk: requires disciplined execution; add inspection mechanism.

### 19. Target Customer Type / OG Analysis

- Source: `xian-yaosheng-co-creation.pptx` slide 12.
- Use for: 目标客户类型.
- Answers: 哪类客户最值得做? 哪类菜系/业态最匹配?
- Needs: customer type, cuisine/business type, sales contribution, Jiale fit.
- Replace with: target customer groups and reasons.
- Risk: avoid overgeneralizing one city to another.

### 20. Strategic Landing Plan

- Source: `xian-yaosheng-co-creation.pptx`; cherry-red slide 113.
- Use for: 共创会落地推进计划.
- Answers: 共识如何变成周/月动作?
- Needs: stage, owner, weekly rhythm, monthly target, inspection metric.
- Replace with: phase plan with owner checks.
- Risk: keep it executable; no abstract roadmap.
