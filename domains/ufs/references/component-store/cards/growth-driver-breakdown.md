# Growth Driver Breakdown

- ID: `growth-driver-breakdown`
- Tier: first
- Source slide: cherry-red Slide 46
- Component role: cause judgment

## Decision Trigger

Use when the page must answer:

增长或下滑到底来自哪里？

## Best Fit

- Sales growth source.
- Jiale growth source.
- Customer contribution decomposition.
- SKU/category contribution decomposition.

## Use When

- The audience needs cause, not just result.
- There are 3-5 drivers that explain the total change.
- Customer, SKU, category, new/recovered, retained, lost, or key-account effects can be separated.

## Do Not Use When

- Only summary KPIs are available.
- Driver data is guessed rather than calculated or explicitly provided.
- The page's real job is customer accountability.

## Required Data

- Current and previous-period sales.
- Growth amount or target gap.
- Driver values, such as new/recovered contribution, retained contribution, lost impact, SKU/category contribution, key-account contribution.
- Optional share of each driver.

## Monthly Review Mapping

- 销售增长来源.
- 家乐增长来源拆解.
- SKU/category contribution page.

## Replacement Method

- Put total change or headline result at the center.
- Branch into 3-5 drivers with amount and explanation.
- Add one management implication: which driver should be protected, expanded, or repaired.

## July Nanchong Example

- 新增/恢复客户贡献约 3.36 万.
- 持续客户贡献约 2.55 万，但相比 6 月略降.
- 6 月有、7 月未复购客户流失约 0.86 万.
- 家乐增长由少数客户和重点 SKU 拉动.

## Risk

It can look convincing while hiding weak data logic. If data is directional rather than exact, label it as directional and do not overstate causality.
