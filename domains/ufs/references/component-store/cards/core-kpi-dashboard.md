# Core KPI Dashboard

- ID: `core-kpi-dashboard`
- Tier: first
- Source slide: cherry-red Slide 102
- Component role: result judgment

## Decision Trigger

Use when the page must answer:

本月核心结果是否达标？老板应该先看哪几个指标？

## Best Fit

- Monthly opening judgment.
- Core KPI overview.
- Owner dashboard.
- Target/actual snapshot before diagnosis.

## Use When

- There are 4-6 owner-level indicators.
- The page must quickly separate good results from hidden risks.
- The next page will explain why the results happened.

## Do Not Use When

- The page needs customer names or SKU detail.
- There are more than 6 metrics.
- The deck already has a dense UFS KPI table that must be preserved exactly.

## Required Data

- Current sales.
- Sales target and completion rate.
- Jiale sales.
- Jiale share.
- Active customers.
- New customers or high-value customers when available.
- Previous month baseline when showing improvement or decline.

## Monthly Review Mapping

- 核心结论.
- 核心指标.
- 7 月核心指标总览.
- 8 月老板看板.

## Replacement Method

- Put one conclusion sentence below the page title.
- Use 4-6 KPI blocks with status tags.
- Add one management implication at the bottom or side.
- Use UFS orange-red and UFS footer/frame; do not turn the full page into a generic consulting dashboard.

## July Nanchong Example

- 全量销售 24.83 万，接近 25 万目标.
- 家乐销售 5.9-6.1 万，超过 5.5 万目标.
- 家乐占比 24%+，明显高于 6 月.
- 活跃客户 112 家，低于 120 家目标.
- 1000 元以上客户 41 家，低于 48 家目标.

## Risk

The component can become a KPI card wall. Keep it as a judgment page: result, risk, and next page direction.
