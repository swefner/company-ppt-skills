# Customer Tier Migration

- ID: `customer-tier-migration`
- Tier: first
- Source slide: cherry-red Slide 87
- Component role: structure judgment

## Decision Trigger

Use when the page must answer:

客户结构有没有变好？哪些客户要保、做深、追回？

## Best Fit

- Customer value tiers.
- Customer migration.
- Risk/opportunity segmentation.
- Customer action classification before detailed lists.

## Use When

- The page must show customer structure, not just names.
- Customers can be grouped by value, growth, decline, Jiale share, or action type.
- Each group has a different management implication.

## Do Not Use When

- The page only needs owner-by-owner task rows.
- Customer groups have no defined threshold.
- Customer names are the main evidence and there are too many to fit.

## Required Data

- Customer name.
- Current sales or Jiale sales.
- Previous-period sales when showing migration.
- Value tier or grouping rule.
- Jiale share when identifying deepening opportunities.
- Owner.
- Action type.

## Monthly Review Mapping

- 客户结构/层级迁移.
- 必须保住的大户.
- 可做深客户.
- 断采/下降风险客户.

## Replacement Method

- Use the component to show the three or four customer groups.
- Use a small supporting list or table for representative customers.
- Add the management rule for each group.
- Put large detailed lists in following pages or appendix.

## July Nanchong Example

- 大户保护：7 家贡献约 4.02 万，8 月至少 5 家复购.
- 做深机会：高总采低家乐客户，至少 6 家提升家乐占比.
- 追回风险：断采/下降客户至少追回 5 家，追回 0.8-1.2 万.

## Risk

It can become decorative segmentation. Always attach thresholds, owners, and next actions.
