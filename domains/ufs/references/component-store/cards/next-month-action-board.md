# Next-Month Action Board

- ID: `next-month-action-board`
- Tier: first
- Source slide: cherry-red Slide 113
- Component role: accountability judgment

## Decision Trigger

Use when the page must answer:

下月老板应该抓哪几件事，怎么追责？

## Best Fit

- Monthly action plan.
- Owner management dashboard.
- Strategic landing plan.
- Responsibility split.

## Use When

- The page must convert analysis into inspectable actions.
- There are 3-5 action themes.
- Every action can have target, owner, metric, and deadline.

## Do Not Use When

- Actions are still vague slogans.
- There are too many actions.
- The page only needs detailed customer rows.

## Required Data

- Action theme.
- Target customer, SKU, channel, or segment.
- Owner.
- Metric and target value.
- Deadline or weekly rhythm.

## Monthly Review Mapping

- 8 月行动计划.
- 8 月老板看板.
- 责任分工与跟进机制.

## Replacement Method

- Top: 3-5 action themes.
- Middle: target, owner, and metric.
- Bottom: inspection cadence or meeting rule.
- Detailed customer rows can sit below or in appendix.

## July Nanchong Example

- 家乐销售保底 6 万，挑战 6.5 万.
- 7 家大户复购数 >= 5 家.
- 高总采低家乐客户提升数 >= 6 家.
- 断采/下降客户追回数 >= 5 家.

## Risk

It can become a plan poster. Every action must be checkable by owner, metric, and time.
