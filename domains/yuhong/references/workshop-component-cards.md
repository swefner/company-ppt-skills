# Workshop Component Cards

Use these cards when creating Yuhong training decks, workshop decks, co-creation meeting decks, or instructor-led courseware.

These are course components. They answer: "How should the learning and co-creation process be structured?" They are different from business-analysis cards and visual component cards.

Primary source: `assets/reference-decks/trader-to-service-provider-course.pptx`.

## Selection Order

1. Choose the course outcome.
2. Choose the workshop component.
3. Define learner input.
4. Define worksheet output.
5. Choose visual layout.
6. Add instructor notes.

## A. Opening And Urgency Components

### A1. Course Gain Contract

- Purpose: Tell learners what this course will give them.
- Best answer: "Why should I spend time on this course?"
- Source pattern: opening page with cases, experience, method, and tool outputs.
- Needed input: course topic, learner type, number of cases, source experience, final deliverables.
- Replace method: Turn the course into tangible gains: cases learned, methods mastered, tools filled, actions decided.
- Output artifact: learning contract page.
- Usage risk: Do not overpromise. Only list tools and outputs that will actually appear later.

### A2. Market Change Pressure

- Purpose: Create urgency for transformation.
- Best answer: "Why is the old way no longer enough?"
- Source pattern: market change and customer change pages.
- Needed input: industry trend, channel change, customer decision change, current dealer pain.
- Replace method: Show past mode vs future mode, then land one strategic conclusion.
- Output artifact: urgency page.
- Usage risk: Avoid abstract macro language. Tie pressure to dealer revenue, margin, customer acquisition, or service cost.

## B. Mindset Transformation Components

### B1. Two-Mindset Contrast

- Purpose: Make an invisible mindset difference concrete.
- Best answer: "What exactly is different between the old and new way?"
- Source pattern: 卖货思维 vs 用户思维; 做贸易 vs 做品牌; 省成本 vs 投未来; 短期主义 vs 长期主义.
- Needed input: old behavior, new behavior, field quote or case, management implication.
- Replace method: Use a sharp left-right contrast and a short quote, then summarize the principle.
- Output artifact: contrast page.
- Usage risk: Do not make the old side foolish or insulting. Many learners are still making money from the old model.

### B2. Hard-First Sweet-Later Curve

- Purpose: Prepare learners for early-stage investment and delayed payoff.
- Best answer: "Why does the right thing feel hard at the beginning?"
- Source pattern: service-provider profitability curve.
- Needed input: expected input period, breakeven signal, later upside, typical pain.
- Replace method: Mark where the learner likely is now and what must be endured or managed.
- Output artifact: expectation-management page.
- Usage risk: Do not promise timeline without data. Use as a mindset model.

### B3. Identity Summary Table

- Purpose: Summarize the transformation into a memorable table.
- Best answer: "What kind of operator do I need to become?"
- Source pattern: trader vs service-provider comparison.
- Needed input: dimensions such as starting point, value core, investment mode, time horizon.
- Replace method: Keep 4-5 rows only, each with a field-language contrast.
- Output artifact: identity table.
- Usage risk: Too many rows will dilute the teaching point.

## C. Strategic Diagnosis Components

### C1. Three-Looks Market Diagnosis

- Purpose: Help learners choose where to fight.
- Best answer: "Which market or channel is most worth attacking first?"
- Source pattern: 看大势 / 看竞争 / 看自我.
- Needed input: user habit, channel trend, channel value, competitor type, self resources.
- Replace method: Teach one lens at a time and ask learners to score or write evidence.
- Output artifact: market opportunity assessment.
- Usage risk: Do not jump from "hot channel" directly to action. Self capability must be considered.

### C2. Channel Value Rating

- Purpose: Compare channels by profit, volume, influence, and fit.
- Best answer: "分销、家装、工长、C 端哪个更适合我?"
- Source pattern: channel comparison pages with 利润空间 / 销量空间 / 影响力.
- Needed input: channel list, expected gross margin, order volume, conversion difficulty, influence value, dealer resources.
- Replace method: Rate each channel and state the first-battle recommendation.
- Output artifact: channel priority table.
- Usage risk: A channel can be attractive but unsuitable for the learner's current team.

### C3. Competitor Response Matrix

- Purpose: Convert competitor analysis into response strategy.
- Best answer: "面对不同对手，我该怎么打?"
- Source pattern: six-dimensional competitor response.
- Needed input: competitor type, price strategy, service level, product mix, construction capability, promotion tactic.
- Replace method: Pick 2-3 real competitor types and define response by dimension.
- Output artifact: competitor response matrix.
- Usage risk: Do not let competitor pages become complaint pages. Every competitor point needs a response action.

### C4. Self-Resource Three Rulers

- Purpose: Assess relationship, experience, and team capability.
- Best answer: "我现有能力最支持哪个渠道?"
- Source pattern: 人脉关系 / 过往经验 / 团队能力.
- Needed input: relationship strength, channel experience, current staffing, owner involvement, delivery capability.
- Replace method: Let learners fill current advantage and weak point by channel.
- Output artifact: self-resource evaluation table.
- Usage risk: Learners often overestimate relationship and underestimate execution. Instructor should challenge evidence.

### C5. Core Battle Map

- Purpose: Convert diagnosis into a strategic choice and action map.
- Best answer: "我第一仗靠什么打赢?"
- Source pattern: strategic positioning and core battle map.
- Needed input: chosen channel, target customer, value proposition, first action, resources, inspection metric.
- Replace method: Force one primary battlefield and one backup, then define the first 30-60 days.
- Output artifact: battle map.
- Usage risk: If every channel is chosen, no strategy has been chosen.

## D. Four Order-Source Components

### D1. Distribution Transfer Model

- Purpose: Teach how to turn distribution outlets into double-package order sources.
- Best answer: "分销网点为什么愿意转单给我?"
- Source pattern: 定价格 / 设激励 / 做推广.
- Needed input: outlet count, existing distribution relationship, package price, referral incentive, promotion materials.
- Replace method: Build the model around value, not only commission: official service, quality assurance, standard process, referral reward.
- Output artifact: distribution package and referral plan.
- Usage risk: If incentives are unclear, outlet behavior will not change.

### D2. Distribution Group Operation

- Purpose: Maintain ongoing outlet motivation.
- Best answer: "怎么让分销网点持续转单，而不是只听一次宣贯?"
- Source pattern: service guarantee group, success case posting, reward reminders, construction proof.
- Needed input: group members, posting frequency, case format, reward rules, responsible operator.
- Replace method: Define who posts what, how often, and how success is celebrated or inspected.
- Output artifact: group operation rule.
- Usage risk: Group operation without real orders quickly becomes noise.

### D3. Home Decoration Company Breakthrough

- Purpose: Build cooperation with home-decoration companies.
- Best answer: "如何从供应商变成家装公司的价值合作伙伴?"
- Source pattern: company screening, boss breakthrough, business follow-up, service方案 design.
- Needed input: company list, size, relationship, service concept, payment quality, key person, current cooperation.
- Replace method: Start with small and medium companies with good relationship and payment quality; assign owner and salesperson roles separately.
- Output artifact: company breakthrough list and communication plan.
- Usage risk: Large companies are attractive but may have high entry cost and payment risk.

### D4. Home Decoration Service Plan Design

- Purpose: Turn service into packages for different company types.
- Best answer: "不同家装公司应该给什么服务方案?"
- Source pattern: competition / control / profit package.
- Needed input: target company segment, price sensitivity, quality demand, service level, warranty, core products, process standard.
- Replace method: Create 2-3方案 types, each with positioning, target company, product, process, service highlight, warranty.
- Output artifact: service package table.
- Usage risk: If方案 only changes price, it is not a service strategy.

### D5. Foreman Activation Model

- Purpose: Build long-term relationship with foremen.
- Best answer: "怎么让工长愿意长期带单?"
- Source pattern: 定激励 / 拉新 / 促活.
- Needed input: foreman list, trade type, region, cooperation intent, referral policy, activity plan, follow-up record.
- Replace method: Combine economic incentive with relationship, circle, technical support, and warm maintenance.
- Output artifact: foreman three-step action plan.
- Usage risk: One-off rebate cannot replace trust and ongoing service.

### D6. C-End Lead Acquisition And Conversion

- Purpose: Teach practical 0-1 C-end acquisition.
- Best answer: "C 端业主从哪里来, 如何转化?"
- Source pattern: community promotion, alliance, online lead, standardized conversion flow.
- Needed input: target communities, alliance partners, promotion materials, lead capture method, conversion roles, quotation/service process.
- Replace method: For 0-1 stage, focus on the quickest and highest ROI paths instead of spreading across all channels.
- Output artifact: C-end lead action plan.
- Usage risk: C-end acquisition without follow-up process wastes leads.

## E. Organization And Risk Components

### E1. Minimum Team Configuration

- Purpose: Make service execution feasible without overbuilding.
- Best answer: "0-1 阶段到底需要哪些人?"
- Source pattern: key task decomposition and role configuration.
- Needed input: monthly order count, task list, current people, owner involvement, delivery model.
- Replace method: Decompose the business process first, then assign current roles and staffing triggers.
- Output artifact: team configuration table.
- Usage risk: Do not prescribe dedicated roles before order volume supports them.

### E2. Role And Process Split

- Purpose: Prevent service work from becoming owner chaos.
- Best answer: "拓单、管家、交付、内勤各管什么?"
- Source pattern: task flow and function grouping.
- Needed input: order process, handoff nodes, responsible role, tools/forms, inspection points.
- Replace method: Define start/end and critical nodes for one full order.
- Output artifact: role-process map.
- Usage risk: If no one owns handoff, service quality will collapse despite good sales.

### E3. Team Incentive And Growth Path

- Purpose: Activate and retain service team members.
- Best answer: "怎么让管家、内勤、师傅持续做好服务?"
- Source pattern: salary, piece-rate, result incentive, quality reward/penalty, growth path.
- Needed input: roles, salary base, order metric, quality metric, training path, certification resources.
- Replace method: Separate process incentive from result incentive and quality discipline.
- Output artifact: incentive design table.
- Usage risk: Incentives based only on volume can hurt quality.

### E4. Financial And Delivery Risk Control

- Purpose: Make double-package service controllable.
- Best answer: "怎么防止交付和回款踩坑?"
- Source pattern: pre-agreement, in-process tracking, post-delivery protection, payment node clauses.
- Needed input: contract/payment rules, delivery milestones, acceptance criteria, receivable record, risk owner.
- Replace method: Build a closed loop: before delivery agreement, during delivery tracking, after delivery acceptance and collection.
- Output artifact: risk-control checklist and contract clause examples.
- Usage risk: Treat clauses as operational examples unless legally reviewed.

## F. Course Assembly Recipes

### Recipe 1. Half-Day Dealer Awakening Workshop

- Use: A1, A2, B1, B3, C1, C5.
- Output: one market judgment and one first-battle direction.
- Avoid: detailed team incentive and risk sections unless requested.

### Recipe 2. One-Day Channel Tactics Workshop

- Use: C1, C2, C5, plus two of D1-D6.
- Output: channel action plan with target list and owner.
- Avoid: teaching all four order sources equally.

### Recipe 3. Two-Day Service Provider Bootcamp

- Use: A1, A2, B1-B3, C1-C5, D1-D6, E1-E4.
- Output: core battle map, channel plans, team plan, risk-control checklist.
- Avoid: overloading visible slide text. Use worksheets and instructor notes.

### Recipe 4. Monthly Review Plus Training

- Use: C2 or C5, one tactical D card, and one E card.
- Output: next-month action plan connected to data review.
- Avoid: turning a monthly review into a full course.

## Proposal Format

When proposing workshop components to the user, use this format:

- Course/workshop goal:
- Selected component:
- Why this component fits:
- Learner input needed:
- Output artifact:
- Suitable page type:
- Instructor note:
- Usage risk:
- Recommendation: Continue / Go deeper / Replace / Build
