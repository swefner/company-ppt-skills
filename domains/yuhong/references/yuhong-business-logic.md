# Yuhong Business Logic

## Consultant Diagnosis Pattern

Use this order:

1. Phenomenon: what changed in the data.
2. Judgment: whether the change is healthy, risky, or incomplete.
3. Evidence: the few metrics or customers/SKUs proving the judgment.
4. Cause: what likely drove the result.
5. Action: what management should do next month.

## Common Monthly Review Questions

- Did sales and collection hit target?
- Was growth driven by more customers, higher customer output, richer SKU mix, or a few large orders?
- Are active customers and new customers improving enough to support sustainable growth?
- Are key products or explosive products becoming deeper in existing customers?
- Is channel work creating measurable transaction results?
- Are visits covering the right customers and producing orders or next actions?
- Which 3-5 management actions should the boss inspect next month?

Before answering these questions, read `metric-definitions.md` when the deck depends on Yuhong-specific terms such as effective store, outlet activity, member activity, home-decoration order, work-order conversion, channel balance, category balance, core product, or explosive product.

## Key Battle Logic

Channel intensive cultivation:
- Diagnose channel coverage, active outlets/customers, channel contribution, and conversion.
- Separate "coverage" from "effective transaction".

Product / explosive product:
- Diagnose product sales by amount, active SKU count, customer penetration, repeat purchase, and key SKU depth.
- Do not treat one large order as product capability unless repeatability is visible.

Retail store / worker member:
- Diagnose member development, member tiering, repeat purchase, referral, and store output.
- Keep the chain: member acquisition -> activation -> purchase -> repeat -> referral.

Home decoration channel:
- Diagnose company development, active home-decoration partners, order count, average order amount, and maintenance rhythm.
- Separate "signed/cooperating" from "monthly active/order-producing".

Trader to service provider:
- Explain changes in organization, customer operation, service capability, and management rhythm.
- Use cases only when they clarify an action, not as decorative storytelling.

## Tone

- Be direct and owner-readable.
- Praise only when the data supports it.
- Use action verbs: 稳住, 做深, 拉动, 复购, 激活, 转化, 追踪, 复盘, 检查.
- Avoid vague slogans such as "全面赋能", "高质量发展", "持续深耕" unless immediately supported by a concrete action.
