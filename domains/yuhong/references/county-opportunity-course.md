## Positioning

Course direction:

- 东方雨虹县域课程.
- 机会诊断 section.
- First two modules: 看趋势 and 判状态.

Core teaching job:

1. Help learners see that county-market opportunity still exists, but the opportunity shape has changed.
2. Help learners restore the real business map of their county, townships, and weak territories.
3. Prepare learners for the next module: 盘区域, where they decide which township or territory deserves priority investment.

## Content Development Principle

The draft explicitly follows this principle:

- 先组织"讲什么", 暂不进入 PPT 分页、版式和详细教学工具设计.
- 降一层语言难度.
- 升一层教学直观性.
- Use long tables and judgment chains to carry information.
- Avoid only listing concept titles.
- Avoid prematurely simulating final PPT pages.

When turning this reference into PPT:

- Preserve the judgment chain.
- Convert dense content into teaching pages, interaction pages, worksheet pages, and instructor notes.
- Do not flatten the material into a decorative card deck.

## Overall Story

The first two modules form one complete logic:

1. 向外看: 政策、市场与消费者发生了什么变化.
2. 向内看: 我的县域市场究竟做到了什么程度.

In one sentence:

先看懂外面发生了什么，再看清自己已经做到哪里。

Recommended course title direction:

- 看清外部变化，还原生意版图.
- 县域机会诊断: 从趋势判断到经营状态还原.
- 县域生意不是没有机会，而是机会位置和打法变了.

## Module 01: 看趋势

Full title:

- 看趋势: 县域机会为什么还在.

Learner question:

- 房地产进入调整期，县城和乡镇的建材生意还有没有机会?
- 如果有，还是过去的新房和铺货逻辑吗?

Teaching purpose:

- Do not broadly say "下沉市场有机会".
- Use national direction, market-center change, and consumption-logic change to explain where opportunity comes from and what it has become.

Core conclusion:

- 县域不是房地产增量市场的简单替代，而是县城、中心镇和乡村之间，新建、翻修、维修与服务需求并存的复合市场。

### 1.1 国家方向

Teaching question:

- 人和资源往哪里走?

Core message:

- 县城和中心镇 continue to carry population, commerce, public service, and infrastructure functions.
- 城市更新, 自建房, 危房改造, 农村人居环境改善, and green building-material policies create dispersed usage scenarios.

Use in PPT:

- Use as an evidence-backed context page.
- Convert policy language into dealer implications.
- Keep only 2-3 policy signals on the visible slide.

Suggested slide forms:

- Signal chain: 国家方向 -> 县域变化 -> 建材机会.
- Three-signal evidence page.
- Ask-then-reveal: "国家资源是不是离开县域了?"

Interaction:

- Ask learners: "你所在县今年最明显的建设/翻修/维修变化是什么?"
- Reveal the three national signals after discussion.

Risk:

- Do not make policy pages too official or abstract.
- Do not imply every county or township will naturally grow.

### 1.2 市场中心变化

Teaching question:

- 生意发生在哪里?

Core message:

- 生意没有消失，发生的位置和方式变了.
- 县城 remains the operating center.
- Demand touches extend toward key townships, villages, self-built housing, renovation, repair, and service projects.

Use in PPT:

- Show the movement from a single real-estate/new-house logic to a composite county-market logic.
- Emphasize that opportunities are unevenly distributed across county town, central towns, and villages.

Suggested slide forms:

- Before/after market map.
- County-center-to-township opportunity chain.
- Scenario table: 新建 / 翻修 / 维修 / 服务.

Interaction:

- Ask learners to name the top 3 places where real orders now appear.
- Compare those places with the course's "county-center / central-town / village-project" logic.

Risk:

- Do not make it a geography lecture. Tie locations to order discovery, recommendation, construction, delivery, and after-sales.

### 1.3 消费逻辑变化

Teaching question:

- 客户为什么选择你?

Core message:

- County and township consumers still care about price.
- But they also fear buying wrong, using wrong,返工, no after-sales, and nobody being responsible.
- The product is not just a bucket of material. The customer buys certainty.

Learner-facing sentence:

- 在县域，客户买的不只是一桶材料，而是"熟人敢推荐、工人会施工、门店能送到、出了问题有人到场"的确定性。

Use in PPT:

- Translate consumption logic into dealer behavior.
- Connect consumer fear to brand, local reputation, construction service, and after-sales response.

Suggested slide forms:

- Consumer fear -> dealer response matrix.
- "Not only cheap" concept contrast.
- Certainty chain: 熟人推荐 -> 会施工 -> 能送到 -> 有人负责.

Interaction:

- Ask: "客户不买你的时候，嘴上说贵，背后真正担心什么?"
- Reveal the certainty chain after learners answer.

Risk:

- Do not deny price sensitivity. The point is that price is not the only decision logic.

### Module 01 Ending

The module should end with three judgments:

1. 国家方向没有离开县域.
2. 建材需求发生结构变化.
3. 过去的卖货方式不够用了.

PPT output:

- A summary judgment page.
- A transition page into Module 02: "外部机会存在，但我的县域到底做到哪里?"

## Module 02: 判状态

Full title:

- 判状态: 还原我的县域生意版图.

Learner question:

- 我的县城和乡镇分别做到什么状态?
- 下一步到底卡在哪里?

Teaching purpose:

- Avoid broadly judging "dealer stage".
- Restore the real situation of county seat, covered townships, and blank/weak townships.
- Help learners distinguish "sold once" from "built a market".

Core judgment:

- 有订单，不等于有市场；能够持续获客、持续卖货，才叫把市场做起来。

### 2.0 Three-Area County Map

Teaching question:

- 先把县域分成哪三类区域看?

Three areas:

1. 县城: 我的大本营稳不稳.
2. 已覆盖乡镇: 是真做了，还是只卖过.
3. 空白或薄弱乡镇: 是没进去，还是没站住.

Use in PPT:

- Build a county business-map page before teaching diagnosis details.
- Separate facts from decisions: first restore what exists, then decide whether to invest.

Suggested slide forms:

- County map framework.
- Three-zone diagnostic table.
- Clickable map menu if making an interactive deck.

Interaction:

- Ask learners to classify their county into three zones.
- If no data exists, let them mark "known / unclear / assumed".

Risk:

- Do not let learners treat every township equally.
- Do not confuse a customer list with an operating network.

### 2.1 Four Forces

Teaching question:

- 不用抽象定义，怎么用四个问题看经营基础?

Four forces:

| Force | Learner-facing question | What to observe | One-sentence meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| 品牌力 | 当地人知不知道、信不信雨虹? | 广告、活动、门店形象、样板、工人推荐、业主口碑 | 有没有人知道，有没有人愿意替雨虹说话 |
| 渠道力 | 当地有没有人卖、有人推? | 稳定网点、工人师傅、施工队、村镇关键人及持续带客情况 | 不是有没有名单，而是这些人是否持续带来生意 |
| 场景力 | 能不能找到活、赶上采购点? | 在建房、待装修、漏水维修、具体项目、施工进度和采购节点 | 能否在客户买材料之前找到他 |
| 产品力 | 先卖什么，能不能越卖越多? | 切入产品、主推产品、重复销售、组合销售和关联扩品 | 从卖进第一款，到围绕同一客户卖一组 |

Learner-facing summary:

- 品牌让人信，渠道帮你卖，场景帮你找活，产品把钱接住。

Use in PPT:

- Present the four forces in field language.
- Use questions before definitions.
- Each force should point to observable evidence.

Interaction:

- Click-to-diagnose matrix: learners choose the weakest force for one township.
- Group vote: "你现在最弱的是哪一力?"

Risk:

- Do not over-theorize four forces.
- If learners cannot answer from field observation, the page has failed.

### 2.2 Four Stages

Teaching question:

- 进得去、站得住、卖得动、做得深分别是什么意思?

Stage principle:

- 四阶段不按开发了几年判断，而是看这个乡镇的市场实际上做到了什么程度。

Four stages:

| Stage | One-sentence judgment | Brand | Channel | Scenario | Product |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 进得去 | 已经找到入口，开始发生第一批业务 | 有初步露出 | 找到第一批关系 | 找到第一批项目 | 卖进第一款产品 |
| 站得住 | 不再只靠偶然订单，开始有稳定基础 | 当地开始知道 | 有稳定网点或合作人 | 能获得一些项目线索 | 产品开始重复销售 |
| 卖得动 | 客户、项目和销量能够持续增加 | 品牌开始带来信任 | 网络逐步铺开 | 能主动、持续找项目 | 主推产品开始上量 |
| 做得深 | 不只追求数量，开始提高单点和单客价值 | 样板口碑形成优势 | 核心伙伴稳定、产出提高 | 复购和转介绍增加 | 从单品走向组合和扩品 |

Use in PPT:

- Use as the main learner self-diagnosis model.
- Pair stages with four forces to avoid vague stage naming.

Suggested slide forms:

- Stage ladder.
- Four-stage x four-force matrix.
- Scenario choice page: "这个乡镇处于哪一阶段?"

Interaction:

- Give a township scenario and ask learners to choose one of four stages.
- Reveal the reasoning: which evidence proves the stage?

Risk:

- Do not judge by calendar time or years of development.
- Do not let learners overstate maturity because there were a few historical orders.

### 2.3 Common Symptoms

Teaching question:

- 有动作但没有形成市场，常见表现是什么?

Teaching method:

- Do not directly tell learners their stage.
- Give a real or realistic operating symptom.
- Ask learners to judge:
  - What symptom is this?
  - Which stage is it near?
  - Which force is weak?
  - What is the next priority task?

Common symptom structure:

| Symptom | Observable behavior | Possible cause | Next priority task |
|---|---|---|---|
| 乡镇都在跑，却没有一个做透 | 多个乡镇都拜访、投广告、找客户，但资源平均分散 | 没有判断乡镇优先级；忽略人口活跃度、建房需求、中心镇辐射能力和配送服务半径 | 先选一个重点乡镇，集中品牌、渠道和人员资源，形成首个据点 |
| 有网点、有陈列，就是不动销 | 门店拿过首批货、挂了门头或做了陈列，此后补货少 | 可能是渠道力、场景力或产品动销动作不足 | 重新判断网点是否能持续带客，并补上动销和场景触达 |

Use in PPT:

- Build this as an interactive diagnosis page.
- Use 3-4 representative symptoms, not a long list.
- Keep "看表现 -> 判状态 -> 找病因 -> 定任务" as the repeated method.

Interaction:

- Risk/diagnosis drill card.
- Small-group case judgment.
- Triggered reveal: show symptom first, reveal possible cause and next task after discussion.

Risk:

- Do not only show success cases.
- Preserve ineffective attempts and "踩坑" sequence when real cases become available.

### 2.4 Homework: County Business Map

Homework title:

- 画出我的县域生意版图.

Homework output:

- 一张能够看出县城基础、乡镇状态和主要短板的县域生意版图。

Learner tasks:

1. 标出县城和主要乡镇.
2. 判断各乡镇属于"进得去、站得住、卖得动、做得深"中的哪种状态.
3. 标出重点乡镇最明显的四力短板.
4. 初步区分需要继续深耕和需要进一步评估的乡镇.
5. 由经销商与雨虹县长课后共同补充真实信息.

Use in PPT:

- Present as homework, not as a complex live data collection exercise in the first two modules.
- Provide a simple example map or table.
- Explain that this output will feed the next module.

Interaction:

- Worksheet walkthrough.
- Optional companion Excel/HTML tool if live collection is needed.

Risk:

- Do not overload this module with full data collection.
- Keep it as a bridge to the next module.
