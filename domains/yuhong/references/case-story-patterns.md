# Case Story Patterns

Use this reference when creating Oriental Yuhong dealer case-sharing decks or borrowing case-story structure for monthly review conclusions. It is based on `assets/reference-decks/rongyu-case.pptx` and `assets/reference-decks/jiabeili-case.pptx`.

Do not copy these cases visually or factually into a new dealer deck unless the user asks. Extract the storytelling pattern.

## Two Case Archetypes

### Archetype A: Company-Operation Upgrade

Reference: Rongyu case.

Core story:
- Business got bigger.
- The owner's personal management capacity became the bottleneck.
- Company-style operation made the business clearer, lighter, and more scalable.

Good for:
- dealer transformation
- organization upgrade
- finance/process/logistics improvement
- service business standardization
- owner-management story

Typical title pattern:
- 生意越做越大，老板却越管越轻松
- 公司化运营转型之路与增长实践

### Archetype B: Full-Channel Market Breakthrough

Reference: Jiabeili case.

Core story:
- Traditional channels alone were not enough.
- The dealer rebuilt growth through channel sinking, home decoration, retail/store, double-package service, and company management.
- Growth came from multiple channels plus operating discipline.

Good for:
- channel intensive cultivation
- county/township penetration
- home-decoration partner growth
- full-category and full-channel operation
- risk-controlled growth

Typical title pattern:
- 全渠道经营的市场突围之路
- 从传统渠道到多端获客、体系化运营

## Opening Thesis

Open with a tension, not a generic title.

Useful opening tensions:
- Business scale is rising, but management pressure is rising faster.
- Traditional distribution has hit a ceiling, so growth must come from channel depth and service transformation.
- The dealer did not win by one tactic, but by making channel, category, service, and organization reinforce each other.
- Growth is valuable only when it can be managed, repeated, and protected from risk.

Avoid:
- "Company introduction"
- "Case sharing"
- "Development history"
- "Business review"

These can appear as section labels, but not as the main thesis.

## Background Setup

Use four background blocks:

1. Region: province, city/district, market type, population/economy or business context when relevant.
2. Market features: city market, township market, building-material market, street stores, competitor pattern.
3. Channel features: distribution outlets, home-decoration companies, retail/store, double-package/service.
4. Starting constraint: what made growth difficult.

Good background phrasing:
- 区域以建材市场为主，乡镇以街边店为主。
- 网点以油漆辅料店、瓷砖店为主。
- 只靠传统渠道越来越难。
- 密集分销下，分销利润越来越薄。
- 盘子变大后，部门增多，个人管理能力遇到瓶颈。

## Development Timeline

Use a timeline to show staged growth. Keep each stage tied to a business change, not just a year.

Rongyu-style timeline:
- 起步期: single category and small scale.
- 转型期: category expansion.
- 加速期: more categories and business lines.
- 全品类运营期: multi-category, service, and management upgrade.

Jiabeili-style timeline:
- 员工期: experience accumulation.
- 踩坑期: entrepreneurship problems and risk exposure.
- 重启期: rebuild customer pool and channels.
- 增长期: double-package and home decoration become growth focus.

Timeline rule:
- Pair every stage with "what changed" and, if available, a number.
- Do not make the timeline decorative.

## Business Distribution Page

Use this page to prove the case has real operating substance.

Include:
- channel split: distribution, retail/store, home decoration/double-package, other.
- outlet/customer count and sales contribution.
- category mix: waterproofing, tile adhesive/mortar, beauty seam, membrane, wall accessories, pipe materials, related lines.
- one synthesis sentence.

Good synthesis sentences:
- 拓新品、叠品类、做双包，扩大生意规模。
- 业绩不断攀升、管理持续精进，才是健康发展之道。
- 渠道+双包，打开全局视野是生意的未来。
- 做服务就是做口碑，做口碑就是做品牌。

## Problem Framing

Frame the case around 2-4 operating bottlenecks.

Rongyu bottleneck pattern:
- 账款算不清、精力跟不上。
- 团队分工乱、工作效率低。
- 物流配送慢、运营成本高.

Jiabeili bottleneck pattern:
- 传统渠道空间有限。
- 乡镇开发不足。
- 家装公司合作有账期和经营波动风险。
- 双包从零散需求到规模化服务，需要标准化管理。

Do not overload with problems. Only include problems that the case later solves.

## Action Page Pattern

Each action page should follow:

Before -> Action -> Now -> Management meaning

Example patterns:

Finance:
- Before: 合伙人代管账务，账目混乱，老板精力被消耗。
- Action: 招聘专职财务，使用系统，固定周/月报表。
- Now: 每周通报应收款，每月出经营报表、收款明细、成本明细。
- Meaning: 老板能清晰掌握各业务线利润点与成本，用数据支撑决策。

Organization:
- Before: 有活一起干，响应慢，服务不精准。
- Action: 梳理组织架构，分层授权管理。
- Now: 施工、专卖、渠道、财务等角色清晰。
- Meaning: 从老板单打独斗转向组织化经营。

Logistics:
- Before: 体量增大后仓储压力大，配送效率低。
- Action: 规划线路、车辆组合、库房选址、装卸工具。
- Now: 半日达，紧急订单更快响应。
- Meaning: 用运营效率支撑销售规模。

Channel sinking:
- Before: 市区客户为主，乡镇开发不足，窜货频发。
- Action: 县城与乡镇专人专管、客户开发、路线拜访、任务追踪、多品类进店。
- Now: 市区与乡镇结构更均衡，渠道下沉带来增量。
- Meaning: 从铺网点变成精细化运营网点。

Home decoration:
- Before: 合作家装公司少，合作以防水切入。
- Action: 拓家装公司、从强势产品切入、延伸产品线、发展维修项目。
- Now: 合作数量提升，材料员/工长/设计师变成资源入口。
- Meaning: 家装公司不只是客户，而是资源池。

Double-package:
- Before: 分销利润变薄，单纯卖材料难增长。
- Action: 从材料转向材料+施工+质保，设报单佣金，建立施工与服务标准。
- Now: 多端获客，订单规模增长。
- Meaning: 服务化提升口碑、品牌和利润空间。

## Risk-Control Page Pattern

Yuhong case language likes "growth with control". When risk appears, do not hide it.

Good structure:
- Cooperation result.
- Risk lesson.
- Hidden gain.
- Operating principle.

Example sentence pattern:
- 账期可以给，但额度必须有；合作可以继续，但风险不能无限放大。
- 家装公司不仅是一个客户，而是一个资源池。风险要控住，但入口不能关掉。

Use risk pages when:
- receivables matter
- home-decoration companies have payment terms
- service/double-package delivery creates quality risk
- growth depends on a few customers or channels

## Management Rhythm Page

Use the rhythm page to show that the case can repeat.

Typical rhythm:
- daily report: shipments, actions, problems.
- weekly meeting: task tracking, problem solving, receivables.
- monthly review: business summary, activity execution, experience sharing, team building.

Good sentence:
- 用日报、周会、月会把动作追踪起来，让问题及时暴露、及时解决。

## Future Plan Page

End with forward-looking but concrete directions.

Good categories:
- full-category operation
- one-stop distribution/service
- double-package + repair service
- company-operation upgrade
- digital and financial management
- team role specialization

Avoid ending with vague "thank you" if the case should teach a method.

## Yuhong-Style Sentence Library

Use these sentence patterns sparingly and adapt to the facts.

- 从单一品类到多品类经营，核心不是多卖 SKU，而是提升单客户产出。
- 渠道下沉带来增量，但真正留下增量的是路线拜访和月度追踪。
- 做服务不是增加成本，而是把材料生意变成口碑生意。
- 老板轻松不是因为少管，而是因为组织、财务、流程开始替老板管。
- 家装公司不仅是客户，也是设计师、工长、材料员资源的入口。
- 账期可以管理，风险不能放任。
- 全品类不是口号，要落到产品组合、客户分层和订单追踪。
- 增长要看销量，更要看结构、回款和可复制动作。
- 公司化运营的价值，是让经验从老板脑子里走到组织流程里。
- 渠道精耕不是多跑几家店，而是分级、定策、追踪、复盘。

## Case Deck Outline Template

Use this 10-12 slide structure for a new dealer case:

1. Opening thesis: the transformation or breakthrough in one sentence.
2. Region and market context.
3. Development timeline.
4. Business distribution and category/channel mix.
5. Core bottleneck or turning point.
6. Action 1: channel/product/service breakthrough.
7. Action 2: organization or finance upgrade.
8. Action 3: logistics/digital/process improvement.
9. Risk-control or hard lesson.
10. Management rhythm and repeatability.
11. Future plan.
12. Closing lesson: what other dealers should copy.

If the case is short, combine slides 6-8 into two action pages.

## Monthly Review Adaptation

When borrowing case patterns for a monthly review:

- Use the case structure for the "why this month matters" story.
- Do not over-biographize the dealer.
- Replace development history with "last month issue -> this month action -> current result".
- Replace case future plan with "next month inspection actions".
- Keep the owner's management implication visible.
