# Business Analysis Cards

These cards are Yuhong business-analysis components, not visual components.

Use them before selecting a page layout from `component-cards.md`. The order is:

1. Choose the operating question.
2. Choose the business-analysis card.
3. Check whether required data exists.
4. Pair it with a visual component.
5. Run `quality-check.md` before building.

Source method decks:

- `assets/reference-decks/channel-intensive-cultivation.pptx`
- `assets/reference-decks/product-battle.pptx`

## A. Channel Intensive Cultivation

### A1. Distribution Growth Formula

- Source: 渠道精耕 method deck.
- Operating question: 分销销售增长到底来自哪里: 覆盖变多、活跃变好, 还是单店产出提升?
- Logic: 销售额 = 覆盖网点数 x 网点活跃率 x 平均单店产出.
- Suitable monthly pages: 核心 KPI 回顾, 7 月目标达成, 销售增长来源, 拜访覆盖效果.
- Required fields: 区域网点总数, 合作网点数, 本月有进货网点数, 网点销售额, 单店产出, 目标值, 上月/同期值.
- How to replace with July review content: Put July sales and target at the top, then decompose into outlet count, active outlet ratio, and average store output. Mark the strongest lever and weakest lever.
- Diagnostic output: This page should say which lever explains the month, not merely show three numbers.
- Visual pairing: KPI scorecard, driver breakdown, or three-column cause card from `component-cards.md`.
- Usage risk: If outlet universe is not defined, coverage rate will be unstable. Use "合作网点数/活跃网点数" instead of "覆盖率" when the total universe is missing.

### A2. Outlet Health Self-Check

- Source: 渠道精耕 method deck.
- Operating question: 网点问题是覆盖不足、活跃不足、单店产出不足, 还是执行管理不到位?
- Logic: Diagnose across coverage, activity, output, policy, route service, data visibility, staff allocation, and sell-through.
- Suitable monthly pages: 客户结构/层级迁移, 拜访覆盖效果, 问题与风险.
- Required fields: 网点清单, 网点等级, 月销售额, 进货频次, 拜访记录, 陈列/铺货标准, 促销执行, 库存/价格检查.
- How to replace with July review content: Select 3-5 high-impact symptoms, then attach one measurable evidence point to each symptom.
- Diagnostic output: Distinguish "现象" from "原因". Example: 活跃率下降 is a result; route service not reaching waist outlets may be a cause.
- Visual pairing: issue matrix, traffic-light health table, or problem-to-action board.
- Usage risk: Do not turn this into a long checklist. Owner pages should only keep issues that change next month's action.

### A3. Outlet Tiering And One-Store-One-Policy

- Source: 渠道精耕 method deck.
- Operating question: 资源是不是投给了最值得经营、最能转化的网点?
- Logic: Core and non-core outlets need different product mix, policy, visit frequency, display standards, and incentive logic.
- Suitable monthly pages: 客户结构/层级迁移, 销售增长来源, 8 月行动计划.
- Required fields: 网点等级, 网点潜力, 销售额, 毛利/回款, 品类数, SKU 数, 拜访频次, 政策投放, 活动执行, 下月目标.
- How to replace with July review content: Show July outlet migration: core stable, waist rising, basic activated, lost/silent needing recovery. Then assign next-month actions by tier.
- Diagnostic output: Identify whether growth quality is healthy. A good month relying only on a few core outlets may still be risky.
- Visual pairing: tier migration, customer pyramid, segmented action table.
- Usage risk: Tiering must not be based only on current sales. Add potential and cooperation quality when possible.

### A4. New Outlet Battlefield

- Source: 渠道精耕 method deck.
- Operating question: 8 月新开网点应该打哪里, 不应该平均撒点?
- Logic: Choose battlefield by township, population, economy, market age, market scale, outlet type, outlet sales, and cooperation potential.
- Suitable monthly pages: 8 月行动计划, 渠道精耕专项, 增长机会.
- Required fields: 乡镇/片区, 区域网点总量, 空白网点, 竞品网点, 市场容量, 历史合作, 潜力判断, 责任业务员.
- How to replace with July review content: Use July blank-market or low-coverage evidence to select 2-4 priority areas, then assign account lists and inspection metrics.
- Diagnostic output: Convert "要开发网点" into "开发哪类网点、在哪个区域、谁去、何时验收".
- Visual pairing: priority matrix, area action map, battle list.
- Usage risk: If no market universe data exists, call it "机会清单" rather than "覆盖率诊断".

### A5. Route Planning And Visit Execution

- Source: 渠道精耕 method deck.
- Operating question: 拜访动作是否真正覆盖了目标网点, 并推动了动销?
- Logic: Scan outlets -> divide territories -> monthly route -> weekly route -> daily route -> one map and two tables -> route ROI.
- Suitable monthly pages: 拜访覆盖效果, 问题与风险, 8 月行动计划.
- Required fields: 拜访计划, 拜访记录, 网点等级, 是否成交/进货, 铺货/陈列/库存/价格检查, 业务员, 路线, 日期.
- How to replace with July review content: Compare planned visits, actual visits, effective visits, and visit-to-order conversion by outlet tier or salesperson.
- Diagnostic output: The point is not "visited many stores"; the point is whether visits reached valuable stores and created measurable movement.
- Visual pairing: funnel, route execution board, salesperson comparison table.
- Usage risk: Route data can be performative. If GPS/photo/order evidence is missing, label conclusions as execution hypothesis.

## B. Product And Explosive Product

### B1. Product Growth Formula

- Source: 产品/爆品 method deck.
- Operating question: 产品增长来自经营品项变多、品项活跃变好, 还是单品产出提升?
- Logic: 销售额 = 经营品项数 x 品项活跃率 x 品项平均产出.
- Suitable monthly pages: 核心 KPI 回顾, 销售增长来源, 家乐 SKU 做深, 产品专项复盘.
- Required fields: SKU/品项清单, 本月销售 SKU, SKU 销售额, 品类, 门店/客户铺货, 回转/复购, 目标, 上月/同期值.
- How to replace with July review content: For July, separate "铺得更广" from "卖得更动" and "核心 SKU 贡献更高".
- Diagnostic output: Explain whether 家乐 is an isolated爆品 result or a repeatable SKU operating method.
- Visual pairing: driver breakdown, SKU contribution bar, category structure card.
- Usage risk: Do not call a SKU "爆品" only because it sold well once. Check coverage, repeat purchase, and margin/collection quality.

### B2. First And Second Curve Product Selection

- Source: 产品/爆品 method deck.
- Operating question: 当前应该守住哪些现金流产品, 同时培育哪些未来增长品?
- Logic: 第一曲线 supports current sales/profit; 第二曲线 builds future sales/profit.
- Suitable monthly pages: 产品结构诊断, 家乐 SKU 做深, 8 月行动计划.
- Required fields: SKU sales, gross margin if available, growth rate, outlet coverage, repeat purchase, product lifecycle, strategic category.
- How to replace with July review content: Put mature high-contribution products and rising products into two groups, then set different August management actions.
- Diagnostic output: Avoid treating all SKUs as one list. The deck should say which SKUs deserve protection, expansion, trial, or pruning.
- Visual pairing: 2x2 matrix, product curve, portfolio table.
- Usage risk: If margin data is absent, do not overstate profit judgment. Use sales contribution and growth as lower-confidence proxies.

### B3. Suitable Outlet Scan And Labeling

- Source: 产品/爆品 method deck.
- Operating question: 家乐或核心品项应该优先铺到哪些网点?
- Logic: Scan by region, outlet type, history cooperation, related category sales, influence, trend, and relationship quality.
- Suitable monthly pages: 家乐 SKU 做深, 客户结构/层级迁移, 8 月行动计划.
- Required fields: 网点类型, 网点等级, 相关品类销量, 当前 SKU 是否铺货, 月销量, 客情, 市场影响力, 流失/沉默状态.
- How to replace with July review content: Build target outlet labels such as 客情店, 声量店, 动销店, 单品店, 待建联店, 流失店, then define different铺货/动销 actions.
- Diagnostic output: Turn a product page into an outlet-action page.
- Visual pairing: outlet label matrix, target account table, priority list.
- Usage risk: Labels must lead to different actions. If every label gets the same policy, the classification is decorative.

### B4. Three-Willingness Promotion Design

- Source: 产品/爆品 method deck.
- Operating question: 为什么网点、业务员、消费者愿意推这个 SKU?
- Logic: 让网点愿意卖, 让业务员愿意卖, 让消费者愿意买.
- Suitable monthly pages: 家乐 SKU 做深, 产品战役复盘, 8 月行动计划.
- Required fields: 网点政策, 业务员激励, 消费者活动, 陈列资源, 培训记录, 试用/首单/复购数据.
- How to replace with July review content: For 家乐, write one action package for each willingness: outlet policy, salesperson push mechanism, consumer scene/benefit.
- Diagnostic output: Explain what made July bright, then convert it into an August repeat mechanism.
- Visual pairing: three-part action card, policy-to-result chain, execution checklist.
- Usage risk: Promotion design easily becomes slogans. Require owner, target outlet, time, and inspection metric.

### B5. Monthly Product Tracking And ROI

- Source: 产品/爆品 method deck.
- Operating question: 产品专项到底有没有跑出可持续投入回报?
- Logic: Track coverage, turnover, growth, cost, and ROI.
- Suitable monthly pages: 产品战役复盘, 目标达成, 问题与风险.
- Required fields: 活动成本, 政策费用, 销售额, 回款, 毛利 if available, 铺货网点, 回转, 增长率, 复购.
- How to replace with July review content: Put 家乐 or core SKU growth beside cost/resource投入, then judge whether to continue, adjust, or stop.
- Diagnostic output: "增长好" is not enough. The page should say whether the growth is worth scaling.
- Visual pairing: ROI card, before-after scorecard, action continuation table.
- Usage risk: When cost data is missing, avoid precise ROI. Use "投入有效性初判" and list missing data.

## C. Retail Store And Worker Member

### C1. Worker Member Growth Formula

- Source: 零售专卖/工人会员 method deck.
- Operating question: 工人会员销售增长来自会员数量、会员活跃, 还是人均产出?
- Logic: 工人会员体系销售额 = 工人会员数量 x 会员活跃率 x 会员人均产出.
- Suitable monthly pages: 核心 KPI 回顾, 零售专卖专项, 客户结构/层级迁移.
- Required fields: 会员总数, 新增会员, 活跃会员, 沉默会员, 会员销售额, 会员订单数, 人均产出, 工种/来源.
- How to replace with July review content: Show July member base, active conversion, and per-member value, then identify the main constraint.
- Diagnostic output: Separate member acquisition from member operation.
- Visual pairing: KPI formula, member funnel, member structure table.
- Usage risk: If only membership count exists without consumption data, do not claim member operation is effective.

### C2. Member Diagnosis

- Source: 零售专卖/工人会员 method deck.
- Operating question: 会员体系卡在哪里: 触达、开发、分级、维系, 还是数据监控?
- Logic: Diagnose hard-to-reach workers, imprecise target development, weak tier management, missing member data, missing sales monitoring, and insufficient routine maintenance.
- Suitable monthly pages: 问题与风险, 零售专卖专项, 8 月行动计划.
- Required fields: 会员来源, 会员档案完整度, 消费记录, 活跃/沉默状态, 维护记录, 活动参与, 责任门店/人员.
- How to replace with July review content: Pick the one or two bottlenecks that most affect August sales, then convert them into member development or activation actions.
- Diagnostic output: The page should tell whether the current priority is "拉新" or "激活".
- Visual pairing: bottleneck card, funnel, action board.
- Usage risk: Member conclusions are weak without consumption and activity records. Mark data gaps clearly.

### C3. Member System Three-Step

- Source: 零售专卖/工人会员 method deck.
- Operating question: 会员体系下一步应该怎么建, 才能从名单变成销售?
- Logic: 会员开发 -> 会员分级 -> 会员运营.
- Suitable monthly pages: 8 月行动计划, 零售专卖专项.
- Required fields: 开发渠道, 会员分级标准, 消费频次, 消费金额, 最近消费时间, 运营动作, 维护频次.
- How to replace with July review content: Define August member development targets, tier rules, and activation scenes. Tie each step to a checkable metric.
- Diagnostic output: Make the member system operational, not just descriptive.
- Visual pairing: three-step roadmap, staged action table, responsibility board.
- Usage risk: Do not build a full会员体系 page if the deck is only a sales review. Use it when member work is a real monthly priority.

## D. Home Decoration Channel

### D1. Home Decoration Growth Formula

- Source: 家装渠道 method deck.
- Operating question: 家装渠道增长来自合作公司数量、活跃公司、订单数, 还是客单价?
- Logic: 家装销售 is driven by cooperating company count, active company rate, average order count, and average order amount; TOP companies and long-tail companies should be separated.
- Suitable monthly pages: 核心 KPI 回顾, 销售增长来源, 家装专项复盘.
- Required fields: 合作家装公司数, 本月报单公司数, 订单数, 销售额, 平均客单价, TOP10 公司销售/订单, 上月/同期值.
- How to replace with July review content: Show whether July growth came from TOP companies deepening, more active companies, or better order quality.
- Diagnostic output: The page should say whether the channel has stable order sources.
- Visual pairing: funnel, TOP10 contribution card, driver breakdown.
- Usage risk: Signed companies are not equal to active companies. Use "本月有报单/有成交" as the active standard where possible.

### D2. Home Decoration Diagnosis

- Source: 家装渠道 method deck.
- Operating question: 家装渠道没有放量, 是触达关键人、目标选择、服务标准、拓单策略, 还是订单监控的问题?
- Logic: Diagnose key-person access, imprecise development targets, weak service standards, weak order expansion strategy, missing order data, and insufficient partner maintenance.
- Suitable monthly pages: 问题与风险, 家装专项复盘, 8 月行动计划.
- Required fields: 家装公司清单, 关键人, 合作状态, 报单记录, 订单金额, 服务动作, 维护记录, 回款/风险记录.
- How to replace with July review content: Put each weak point beside one operational evidence item, then pick 2-3 actions for August.
- Diagnostic output: Convert broad "家装要加强" into a precise bottleneck.
- Visual pairing: diagnosis matrix, problem-to-action board.
- Usage risk: Do not blame家装公司 "意愿不强" without evidence from visit, order, or key-person records.

### D3. Partner Development And Active Company Management

- Source: 家装渠道 method deck.
- Operating question: 哪些家装公司值得继续深挖, 哪些需要激活, 哪些只是名单?
- Logic: Separate target companies, signed/cooperating companies, active companies, TOP contributors, and silent companies.
- Suitable monthly pages: 客户结构/层级迁移, 家装专项复盘, 8 月行动计划.
- Required fields: 公司等级, 合作状态, 月订单数, 月销售额, 客单价, 最近报单时间, 维护人, 关键人关系, 潜力判断.
- How to replace with July review content: Build a company list by action type: deepen TOP, activate silent, develop target, recover lost.
- Diagnostic output: Make the channel manageable by company stage.
- Visual pairing: stage funnel, customer tier table, August action list.
- Usage risk: If the user has only total家装销售, do not overbuild this page. Ask for company-level order data or use a lightweight hypothesis page.

### D4. Order Quality And Risk Control

- Source: 家装渠道 method deck.
- Operating question: 家装订单增长是否高质量: 客单、回款、复购、风险是否可控?
- Logic: Sales growth must be checked against order amount, frequency, payment, repeat cooperation, and risk exposure.
- Suitable monthly pages: 目标达成, 问题与风险, 家装专项复盘.
- Required fields: 订单金额, 客单价, 回款, 账期/授信, 复购, 退单/投诉, 责任人.
- How to replace with July review content: Compare order growth with collection and risk signals. Keep the conclusion simple: scale, adjust, or control.
- Diagnostic output: Tell the owner whether to keep pushing volume or tighten controls.
- Visual pairing: quality scorecard, risk table, decision card.
- Usage risk: If receivable and margin data are absent, avoid judging profit quality.

## Selection Rules

- If the page is about "where growth came from", start with a formula card.
- If the page is about "why execution did not convert", start with a diagnosis card.
- If the page is about "what to do next month", start with an action or tiering card.
- If the page is about 家乐 or another SKU, do not start from a generic product page. Start from B1, B3, or B4.
- If data is weak, use the card as a hypothesis structure and explicitly name missing fields.
- Do not use more than two deep business-analysis cards in one monthly review section unless the user asks to go deeper.
